# Pícaro de Elociencia: colores y formas

Cuatro propuestas exploratorias generadas con la herramienta integrada imagegen a partir del mapache pícaro seleccionado. Dos pruebas de paleta conservan su pose; dos exploran variantes de silueta en negro, naranja y marfil. PNG con fondo transparente; no se integraron en la app y los originales se conservaron.

## Prompt común

```text
Use case: identity-preserve logo avatar color and shape exploration.
The attached raccoon is the CLIENT-SELECTED "pícaro" avatar for Elociencia, a smart and witty Spanish-language League of Legends learning coach. Create a considered variation of THIS SAME CHARACTER. Preserve its recognisable raccoon face-mask, round ears, ONE circular scientific lens with a short minimal strap, relaxed knowing eye and subtle cheeky asymmetrical smile. It should feel like a clever friend who explains your mistakes with humor. Preserve mature, confident graphic restraint: not a baby plush mascot, aggressive esports crest or existing commercial cartoon animal.

Produce ONE standalone avatar, no wordmark, text, labels, layout panels, frames, app tile, extra characters, bodies or props. True transparent background. Square image, generous breathing space, character fills about 75%. Very clean flat vector-like shapes, strong silhouette, a few purposeful face planes, crisp edges, absolutely no gradients, texture, shadows, reflections, glossy effects, lighting or 3D. All background pixels transparent. Original independent animal design, no imitations of Riot assets or recognizable commercial mascots. The requested palette and shape change below override the reference's colors and orientation only as specified.
```
