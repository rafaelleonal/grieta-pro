# Pícaro de Elociencia: variaciones de azul

Tres propuestas generadas con la herramienta integrada imagegen a partir del mapache pícaro seleccionado. Mantienen negro y marfil y exploran cobalto, acero y cielo en los acentos. PNG con fondo transparente; no se integraron en la app y los originales se conservaron. Los códigos del prompt son objetivos de color, no una especificación vectorial verificada del raster generado.

## Prompt común

```text

Strictly preserve its head silhouette, proportions, three-quarter right-facing pose, cheeky grin, relaxed eye, circular ivory scientific lens, short strap and recognisable raccoon face-mask. This is an ACCENT COLOR change only, not a redesign. Keep every existing black fur, eye-mask, nose, mouth and pupil ink-black #141414. Keep every existing light face plane, lens and eye warm ivory #F3EBDD. Change ONLY the existing orange inner-ear and cheek-accent shapes to the requested BLUE hue. Use exactly the same blue consistently in both places. All dark regions remain BLACK, never dark blue; all light regions remain IVORY. No orange, purple, teal, green or other hue. No added elements.

One standalone avatar, no wordmark, text, labels, app tile, frames, panels or extra objects. Square composition and placement consistent with the reference, character about 75% of the canvas. True transparent background, preserving alpha. Crisp flat vector-like solid shapes. No gradient, shadows, lighting, highlights, texture, gloss, glow or 3D. Preserve the selected character's intelligent gently amused personality.
```
