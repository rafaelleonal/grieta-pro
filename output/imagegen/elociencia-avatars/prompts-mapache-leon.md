# Mapache y león de Elociencia

Alternativas exploratorias generadas con la herramienta integrada imagegen, tomando como referencia el cuervo simplificado aprobado. PNG con fondo transparente; no se integraron en la app. Los originales se conservaron.

## Prompt común

```text
Use case: logo-brand avatar variation.
Create one additional original animal avatar for Elociencia, a Spanish-language game-learning coach. Match the attached APPROVED simplified crow's exact identity system: restrained ink-black silhouette, burnt-orange accent, warm ivory negative space, ONE clean circular scientific protective lens, dryly amused observant personality. Create ONE standalone animal avatar, without brand name, letters, text, framing, app tile, labels, extra symbols or other animals. Genuinely transparent background. Compact balanced square composition, avatar filling about 75% of the canvas. Crisp flat vector-like artwork, around five or six carefully chosen bold shapes, clear species-specific silhouette, readable at 32px. No gradients, lighting, shadows, shading, textures, glossy effects, 3D, background or decorative outlines. One warm-ivory round lens with a short minimal bridge/strap detail sits over the visible eye, no bulky ski goggles or reflections. Small ivory eye and dark pupil, relaxed upper eyelid, no angry heavy eyebrows. Calm, smart, gently mischievous companion; not a baby plush character or an aggressive esports emblem. Keep ink-black dominant, one or two burnt-orange accent shapes, ivory details. Entirely original anatomy and composition, not imitating any existing commercial mascot, Riot assets, champions, minions, esports team logos or animated-film characters.
```
