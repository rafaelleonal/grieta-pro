# Avatares de Elociencia

Tres alternativas exploratorias generadas con la herramienta integrada **imagegen**, tomando como referencia la propuesta de cuervo simplificada aprobada. PNG con fondo transparente; no se integraron en la app. Los originales generados se conservaron.

## Prompt común

```text
Use case: logo-brand avatar variation.
The client approved the attached Elociencia logo's restrained graphic treatment: simple ink-black silhouette, burnt-orange accent and one clean ivory circular protective lens, with an observant gently amused personality. Create an ALTERNATIVE ORIGINAL ANIMAL AVATAR replacing the crow, preserving this exact visual spirit. Deliver ONE standalone avatar, WITHOUT wordmark, letters, text, framing, app tile, presentation panels or extra symbols. The avatar should fill roughly 75% of a square composition and have a genuinely TRANSPARENT background. Crisp flat vector-like artwork, around five or six bold simple shapes, excellent clear silhouette, purposeful negative space and readability at 32px. No gradients, shading, textures, outlines around every shape, shine, shadows, 3D or background. Calm intelligent companion, not angry, babyish, sugary, aggressive or esports styling. One thin but clearly readable ivory circular safety lens with a short minimal bridge/strap detail over the visible eye; no bulky ski goggles or reflections. Tiny ivory eye and dark pupil, slightly relaxed eyelid, no giant eyebrows. Only ink-black, burnt-orange and warm ivory. Entirely original character anatomy and proportions, not resembling Riot champions, poros, minions or any existing commercial mascot. Preserve the sophistication and simplicity of the approved crow, while making the new animal clearly recognisable.
```

## Pulpo estratega

Archivo: `elociencia-pulpo-v1.png`.

```text
Subject: an ORIGINAL OCTOPUS avatar in a compact three-quarter view facing right: a slightly elongated black mantle/head above exactly THREE visible short sweeping tentacle silhouettes that curve together into an exceptionally simple balanced emblem. One tentacle has a small burnt-orange terminal curve. One visible tiny ivory eye with dark pupil behind a clean round ivory protective scientific lens and a very short minimal strap suggestion. Quietly clever, observant, gently amused expression; no smiley baby face or angry eyebrow. No suckers, extra limb details, giant cartoon eyes or complicated curls. Avoid Splatoon characters, Squidward, Kraken gaming marks and all existing commercial mascots. This is the coach who thinks about several decisions at once.
```

Estas son propuestas raster. La petición de originalidad en el prompt no equivale a una comprobación de marcas; la lectura a tamaños pequeños y la vectorización definitiva se revisan al seleccionar una propuesta.
