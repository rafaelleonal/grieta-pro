# Variaciones del mapache de Elociencia

Cuatro propuestas exploratorias generadas con la herramienta integrada imagegen a partir del mapache seleccionado. PNG con fondo transparente; no se integraron en la app y los originales se conservaron. Las tres primeras mantienen la vista lateral; la cuarta explora una vista frontal más distinta.

## Prompt común de las primeras tres

```text
Use case: identity-preserve logo avatar refinement.
Refine the attached ORIGINAL RACCOON avatar for Elociencia, a witty Spanish-language League of Legends learning coach. The client selected this raccoon and now wants variations of this SAME character, not another animal or an unrelated brand. Preserve its recognizable raccoon eye-mask, small rounded ears, right-facing three-quarter orientation, ONE circular scientific lens with a short simple strap, ink-black dominant silhouette, warm ivory facial planes, burnt-orange accents, and clever gently amused personality. Create ONE standalone avatar only, no wordmark, text, labels, app tile, frame, panels, props or extra characters. Genuinely transparent background. The avatar fills about 75% of a compact balanced square. Professional crisp flat vector-like graphic design, a few smooth bold shapes, readable at small size; no gradients, textures, glow, shadows, lighting, 3D or complex fur details. Avoid heavy angry eyebrows, generic aggressive esports styling, cartoon baby proportions, and resemblance to familiar commercial raccoons. Preserve the character identity of this reference while carefully applying the specified variation.
```

## Mapache compacto

Archivo: `elociencia-mapache-compacto-v1.png`.

```text
Variation A — compact silhouette and excellent icon readability. Shorten the snout slightly, round the nape into one clean smooth curve, and reduce the neck/cheek tufts to ONE restrained notch. Make the two round ears a little smaller and more balanced. Keep the eye fully open with a calm attentive expression and no decorative highlight. Simplify the ivory face-mask to two broad clear shapes. Put orange only inside the nearer ear, a single small accent; the nose stays black and the cheek stays ivory. Keep the lens a clean ivory circle, with simple consistent weight. The result should feel like the same raccoon, freshly distilled into a strong minimal app avatar, friendly but not overly cute.
```

## Mapache pícaro

Archivo: `elociencia-mapache-picaro-v1.png`.

```text
Variation B — witty personality and a subtle knowing grin. Preserve the reference's slightly elongated snout, distinctive raccoon mask and relaxed visible eye, but gently lift one end of the mouth into a tiny asymmetrical smile. Use a slight head tilt and a relaxed upper eyelid to suggest 'I know why you died in lane', without angry eyebrows, a sneer or an aggressive expression. Keep orange only as a small curved cheek accent and the near inner ear. Simplify the silhouette to one smooth nape with at most two small fur notches, no spikes. Retain a simple ivory circular lens. This is the clever teasing companion, expressive through just the eye and mouth, with the same identity as the original.
```

## Mapache de perfil

```text
Variation C — an elegant quieter profile and very simple geometry. Turn the SAME raccoon into a clearer near-side profile facing right, showing one small round ear prominently and only a subtle suggestion of the farther ear. Use a graceful tapered snout, tiny black nose and a compact curved neck. ```
```

## Mapache frontal

Archivo: `elociencia-mapache-frontal-v1.png`.

```text
Use case: identity-preserve logo avatar refinement.
Create a CLEARLY DIFFERENT FRONTAL icon variation of the attached approved Elociencia raccoon, retaining its identity and palette. The reference is for character, colors and graphic technique ONLY. Change the VIEWPOINT decisively: exactly front-facing, centered, two visible eyes, two small rounded ears, head only, no neck. Do NOT produce another right-facing three-quarter view or profile.

An adult raccoon head with a compact slightly hexagonal contour tapering toward the chin, two modest round ears, a broad warm-ivory forehead and muzzle separated by a simple horizontal black raccoon eye-mask. One circular ivory scientific monocle lens over the LEFT eye as viewed by the audience, with a very short tiny strap; the right eye has no lens. Both eyes are small ivory shapes with dark pupils, calm alert expression and a subtly asymmetric restrained smile. A tiny centered black triangular raccoon nose, short snout. ONE small burnt-orange inner-ear patch, and no orange cheek stripe. The animal is clever and friendly, not babyish or angry; no arched eyebrows. Maintain the exact ink-black, ivory and burnt-orange identity of the original raccoon.

Extremely clean compact design in no more than seven or eight bold deliberate flat shapes. Contour primarily symmetric except for the monocle and small smile. No tufts, fur details, highlights, gradient, shadows, textures, glossy effects, 3D, thick outlines around every shape or background. Very high clarity at small icon sizes. Genuinely transparent background, square composition, head filling about 75% of the canvas. ONE avatar only, no wordmark, letters, text, logo presentation board, app tile, border, body or objects. Entirely original independent character, not an existing commercial raccoon.
```
