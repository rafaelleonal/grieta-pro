# Pícaro de Elociencia: variaciones de naranja

Tres propuestas generadas con la herramienta integrada imagegen a partir del mapache pícaro seleccionado. Mantienen negro y marfil y exploran naranja quemado, mandarina y ámbar en los acentos. PNG con fondo transparente; no se integraron en la app y los originales se conservaron. Los códigos del prompt son objetivos de color, no una especificación vectorial verificada del raster generado.

## Prompt común

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit — logo palette refinement.
Edit the attached CLIENT-SELECTED Elociencia "pícaro" raccoon avatar. This is strictly an accent-color study, NOT a redesign. Preserve the exact recognizable character, head outline, ear shapes, three-quarter right-facing pose, cheeky smile, relaxed eye, raccoon mask, circular ivory scientific lens and short strap. Keep ALL black fur, dark face-mask, nose, pupil and mouth in ink-black (#141414). Keep ALL light face planes, lens and eye in warm ivory (#F3EBDD). Do not introduce purple, teal, blue, lime or any other hue. Change ONLY the existing orange inner-ear and cheek accent shapes to the requested orange below, consistently using the same orange in both places. No added shapes or accessories.

One standalone avatar only, no text, labels, wordmark, panels, framing, app tile or extra elements. Square balanced composition, matching the reference proportions and placement as closely as possible. True TRANSPARENT background. Crisp clean flat vector-like graphic appearance, solid color fills, absolutely no gradients, shading, textures, glossy effects, highlights, shadows, lighting or 3D. Preserve the original and produce a new sibling variation.
```

## 1 · Naranja quemado

Use a warm deep BURNT ORANGE accent, hex #C85A20. Orange should feel rich, earthy and saturated, not red, brown or pale peach.
